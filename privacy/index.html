Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

This page covers "outreach", a private tool I built for my own freelance work. It is not offered to anyone else and no one else can sign in to it.

What it accesses. With my permission, it uses the Gmail API on my own Gmail account to create email drafts and to check whether a draft it created still exists. It does not read my inbox, and it never sends email. I review and send every draft myself.

What it stores. Business contact details that companies publish themselves (company name, website, and a work email address) plus the status of each draft, kept in a private repository I control.

Sharing. Nothing is sold or shared. Website text is processed by an AI model on Amazon Bedrock to help draft a message; Google user data from Gmail is never used to train models.

Your choice. If I've emailed you and you'd rather I didn't, reply "no" and I won't email again. For anything else: ajiferukeolatommy@gmail.com.
